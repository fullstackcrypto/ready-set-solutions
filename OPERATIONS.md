# Ready-Set Solutions — East Valley Client Acquisition & Billing Playbook
Updated November 17, 2026. Internal operating guide; do not publish customer information in this public GitHub repository.

## Positioning
**One-line pitch:** Local, veteran-owned cleanup and recurring exterior property care in Chandler and the East Valley. Written scopes, before/after photos, and straightforward invoices.

**Three services:** Quick one-time cleanup; rental turnover prep; recurring visual property checks and light exterior upkeep.

1. **Chandler** — closest routing, initial route density and first Google Business Profile service area.
2. **Gilbert** — adjacent homeowners, rental turns and property managers.
3. **Tempe** — landlords, tenants between leases, property-manager partnerships.
4. **South/Central Mesa** — rental turnovers and exterior cleanup.
5. **Ahwatukee / Sun Lakes** — planned recurring visits and owner-away property checks.
6. **Queen Creek** — quote travel separately; add planned days when demand justifies driving.

This order is an operational hypothesis, not a verified lead-volume ranking. Track customer leads, job cost, drive time, conversion and repeat bookings by ZIP code before expanding.

## 30-day outreach sprint
**Week 1:** Confirm contact form delivers to readyset@charleysllc.com; set up or update Google Business Profile with truthful category/service area and genuine photos. Prepare a one-page service sheet, W-9, certificate of insurance if held, and a professional quote template.
**Week 2:** Contact 5 property managers or agents per business day. Ask about approved-vendor requirements and offer a small permitted trial cleanup with photo documentation. Record results in a private pipeline.
**Week 3:** Post one genuine before/after case study with permission. Ask completed customers for honest Google reviews; do not offer incentives conditioned on rating. Follow up with previously contacted managers.
**Week 4:** Compare lead sources, job profit after travel and materials, repeat requests, and unpaid invoices. Keep the top two acquisition channels. Suggest an ongoing plan only where the work is profitable and within permitted scope.

## Simple business paperwork and Stripe
A Ready-Set Solutions Stripe account is already connected. Prefer Stripe Dashboard hosted **invoices**, rather than new website checkout code.

1. **Quote:** provide written scope: exact area, labor, excluded services, materials, third-party disposal costs if relevant, estimated date, customer approval.
2. **Work order:** keep approved quote and job contact details in private records; confirm site access, parking, water/electric availability if used.
3. **Completion:** send before/after photos where appropriate with privacy and customer permissions in mind; note any scope changes that were approved.
4. **Invoice:** create a customer and itemized invoice in Stripe Dashboard using the approved terms, job reference, date and payment due date. Send the Stripe-hosted invoice by email. Never collect card numbers in the website form. Stripe payment links may work for specific approved fixed-price jobs, but don't replace itemized estimates.
5. **Follow-up:** use Stripe's invoice status/reminders; verify receipt for bank payments and reconcile against your job record. Do not auto-charge, auto-subscribe, or create new invoices without a specific approved customer/job.

**Suggested draft invoice template:**
- Line 1: Approved property cleanup labor — area and agreed task description.
- Line 2: Preapproved materials, if any.
- Line 3: Authorized third-party hauling / disposal, if applicable.
- Footer: Ready-Set Solutions, operated by CHARLEY'S LLC. Customer-approved quote [reference]. Please reply with any invoice questions.
- Never represent a refundable deposit, payment terms, or a discount as agreed unless actually agreed.

**Recurring service prompt:** “Would a scheduled exterior walk-through with photo updates be useful for this property? I can quote a repeat schedule based on the property and frequency.”

## Compliance and factual checks
- Verify Arizona ROC status before any representation of licensing. Unlicensed contractors advertising exempt services in Arizona must use the exact phrase **Not a Licensed Contractor**. Arizona's handyman exemption has specific total-cost and permit limits; review ROC guidance instead of assuming all property-maintenance services are exempt.
- City of Chandler commercial waste hauling requires a permit, and the municipal residential recycling/solid waste center is not a default business disposal site. For hauling, quote an authorized third-party provider until applicable permits are secured.
- Do not promise full interior cleaning, mold or biohazard remediation, electrical, irrigation construction, demolition, structural work, or commercial hauling unless properly authorized, staffed, and insured.
- Validate starting prices, public phone number, FormSubmit activation and capacity before advertising aggressively.
- Existing website's older review/rating claims were removed because their verifiability could not be established.
- Never place secret API keys or customer documents in public GitHub.
